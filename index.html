THE IDEA

Built around music.

Tjim 1 is being designed as a music-focused AI, rather than just another chatbot.

🧠

Understands ideas

Tjim 1 will interpret descriptions such as mood, genre, instruments, energy and atmosphere.

🎼

Musical structure

The goal is to create structured music with melodies, chords, rhythm and sections.

🎹

Instruments

Piano, synthesizers, drums, bass and other instruments can become part of a generated track.

🎮

Game music

Imagine describing the atmosphere of your game and creating music around that idea.

🎬

Cinematic music

Tension, adventure, discovery and dramatic moments can all become musical directions.

⚡

Simple interface

The goal is to make music creation feel as straightforward as writing a sentence.
