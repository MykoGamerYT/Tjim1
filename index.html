The experience

Built for creators who think in sound.

The concept is simple: describe a feeling, shape a scene, and let the AI turn it into music-making direction.

The idea

Everything starts with the song.

Tjim 1 is designed to understand musical ideas, instruments, pace, emotion and structure in one creative flow.

🧠

Understands intent

Interpret mood, genre, energy and atmosphere from simple natural language prompts.

🎼

Musical structure

Instrument-first design

Game soundtracks

Cinematic music
